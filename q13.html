13. Multilingual Text Rendering

The <meta charset="UTF-8"> tag in the head section ensures that all characters from different languages are correctly displayed by the browser.

English

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. This is a standard test sentence.

Hindi (हिंदी)

हर इंसान को सम्मान के साथ जीने का हक है। (Har insaan ko samman ke saath jeene ka haq hai.)
Meaning: Every person has the right to live with dignity.

French (Français)

Où est le bibliothèque? J'adore l'été. (Where is the library? I love summer.)
Note the use of accented characters like 'é' and 'ô'.
